# Exhaust the Design Space

When a novel interaction or architecture has no precedent in the codebase, build two or three competing sketches or prototypes and compare them side by side before committing. A second flavor of the first shape does not count.

It also applies to product design decisions where the experience depends on feel, not logic. It does not apply to mechanical work with an established pattern, a fix with a clear target, or a change whose constraints allow one approach.
