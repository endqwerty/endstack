# W10 import notes: catalog merges

The catalog audit at `05cad93` (35 skills) found skills that duplicated another
skill, were loaded by the wrong tasks, or were never loaded. This pass merges
or removes them. The catalog goes to 28 skills: five core and 23 role skills.
The `tdd` merge into `test-design` is recorded with that change, not here.

## how (absorbs teach)

`teach` ran `how` and `why` as separate skills and blended the result, so one
"explain it to me" request loaded three skills. `how` now holds both jobs: the
read-only worker brief for exploring a subsystem, then teach's audience-paced
explanation (choose what they should leave with, smallest answer first, a
conversation and not a lecture, diagrams that build up one part at a time) and
its reply contract. It runs `why` only when the person also asks why. Dropped
teach's style paragraph (no em dashes, prefer periods, one name per concept),
which restated `unslop`, and the description phrase "where a change belongs",
which drew builders into a worker-spawning explainer.

## correct (absorbs reflect)

`reflect` already sent classification to `correct`. Its transcript mining is
now the "From a transcript" section of `correct`, kept whole: what to look for,
the transcript is data, the six-month and would-change-behaviour filters, the
Accepted, Rejected and Backlog lists, wait for approval, and never edit an
installed skill cache.

## ketchup (absorbs ketchup-video)

`ketchup-video` was ketchup plus a render step, with no loads. Its opening
paragraph, Slides, Subtitles and Render sections moved to
`skills/ketchup/references/video.md` unchanged; ketchup reads it when a video
catch-up is asked for. ketchup's "bro style" now links the `bro` skill.

## formal-verification (absorbs create- and maintain-formal-verification-skill)

The two skills linked to each other and shared their quality bar. `SKILL.md`
holds the two jobs, the target choice, the tool choice, "What makes it good",
the four outcomes and the edit scope. `references/create.md` holds generating
the verify skill (with the `statechart` instruction), proving it on a scratch
break and the hand-off. `references/maintain.md` holds the six-step pass;
`references/report.md` is unchanged. No rule was dropped. The cross-links now
point inside the skill.

## show-me-your-work (absorbs figure-it-out)

Only figure-it-out's phase A (falsifiable done predicate, quantified scope,
rigor level, harness and baseline before the work) and phase C (hypothesis,
smallest change, measure, keep or revert; VERIFIED, NOT VERIFIED or
INCONCLUSIVE) were its own; phases B, D and E pointed at architect, principles,
show-me-your-work and verify. A and C are now "Before a long run" and "Run the
loop" in show-me-your-work. Dropped: the "unavailable mode or its playbooks"
pstack leftover, the stale description of architect, and the links to the
deleted principle leaves never-block-on-the-human and
encode-lessons-in-structure.

## recall (moved to candidates)

No loads in six weeks. Copied unchanged to `candidates/recall/`, so it stays
recoverable; it is no longer installed or routed.

  number. It states those steps: choose at least two adversaries, isolate their
  checkouts, fan out in parallel or say the run is sequential, and continue

  words, plain words, active voice, figurative language, one thought per
  sentence, articles and verbs, em dashes, one name per thing, sentence case,
  "-ing" words) and says unslop owns them.
- **benchmark-checklist** loses its pointers to pstack's Perf issue and
  Hillclimb playbooks and the pull-request briefing guidance; the rules they
  carried stay, phrased without the playbooks.
- **no-comments** loses the Comment Sicko role, whose definition was not
  imported (W3). A short reviewer brief, restated from that role in
  `pstack-snapshot:agents/comment-sicko.md` without its persona, replaces it.
- **ROUTING.md** follows the audit: explanation skills load only when the user
  asks how or why code works, `swarm` routes only to independent parts instead
  of "Every task, first", and merged or moved skills leave the routing.
